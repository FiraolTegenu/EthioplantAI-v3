Our Story

Rooted in Ethiopia,
growing with AI

EthioPlant AI began as one student's answer to a question his father asked in the field: what plant is this, and is it sick? We're building the tool — and the database — to answer that, together.

Why we exist

A database Ethiopia didn't have yet

Global plant-identification tools are trained mostly on flora from Europe and North America. Ethiopia's native plants, crops, and the diseases that affect them are underrepresented — which means farmers and researchers here often get a shrug where they need an answer.

EthioPlant AI pairs PlantNet's identification engine with a growing, community-built gallery of Ethiopian plant photos while aiming to train a model based on the database in the future.

What the platform does

Upload a plant photo and get it identified. Upload a photo of affected leaves, stems, or fruit and get a disease read. Every submission is saved to the public gallery — no sign-in required.
